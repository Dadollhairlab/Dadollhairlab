DaDollHairLab Booking Site

This version uses FormSubmit for email notifications:
- Customer submits the booking form.
- FormSubmit emails the booking details to bazarriah@icloud.com.
- On the first live submission, FormSubmit may send an activation/confirmation email to the owner. Complete that activation so future bookings are delivered.
- The form includes the selected service, date, time, client name, phone, email, notes, payment method, and $10 deposit requirement.

Calendar:

- Past dates are disabled.
- Time choices appear after a date is selected.
